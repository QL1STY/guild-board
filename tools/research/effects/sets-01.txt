# R5: artifact set effects, newest sets first as inventory.json lists them. The condition's first word is the piece count
# (2 or 4) the effect belongs to. An element's own DMG Bonus (Cryo DMG Bonus +15%) is `dmg`, `met`, for a character of that
# element, as the plan's `dmg` is the DMG Bonus of her element; Physical DMG Bonus is `phys` (the plan's
# amendment of 2026-10-29), which the engine counts only for a physical character. Effects on other party members, reaction DMG, RES shred, Energy and Shield Strength are
# not stats of the wearer, so the notes list them.
